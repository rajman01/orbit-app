| | |
|---|---|

## Goal

Make Orbit safe and pleasant for people other than its owner, if and when that's wanted.

## Scope

- Onboarding polish for new users; sample content and guided first week.
- Per-user AI usage limits and cost tracking; abuse protection.
- Billing (if desired).
- Privacy policy, terms, data export and account deletion (SET-7) reviewed for public use.
- Admin tooling: user lookup, support actions, health dashboards.
- Google OAuth app published and verified; Calendar scopes verified.
- Rate limits, monitoring and alerting reviewed for many users.
